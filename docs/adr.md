> **Spoiler policy:** ADRs record *that* a decision was made and its consequences, never the
> plot content itself. Story specifics live in `bible/` (spoilers) — deliberately not here.

Rationale: the author asked for the smallest possible number of prompts; the gate + ledger make unattended chaining safe. | accepted |
| ADR-007 | 2026-08-04 | Dramatic irony over surprise for the late reveal — the interlude frame hands the attentive reader its key at roughly one quarter of the book, and the final chapter is written as *recognition*, not shock. Confirmed deliberately after the part-I blind audit reported the key as already held (80 % confidence) and the part-III/full-book audits reported the recognition reading as the stronger one. Consequence for P2: the closing chapter must never be re-cut toward a twist beat. | accepted |
| ADR-008 | 2026-08-04 | Length landed at ~53,000 words against the 57,200 budget (−7 %); accepted rather than padded. Every unit sits inside its hard band, all three review panels reported no thin chapter, and the blind full-book audit called the manuscript "remarkably free of fat". Any growth belongs to P2 depth passes, never to filler written to hit a number. | accepted |
| ADR-009 | 2026-08-04 | P2 editorial posture — "polish, no surgery". Four independent full-book cold reads (pacing · readability · pull · structure) all returned the same verdict, so the edit pass was scoped to line-level subtraction and clarification: no scene added, no chapter restructured, no unit re-ordered, no ledger row touched. Two book-wide operations were run deliberately: thinning the aphoristic paragraph-ending cadence (flagged convergently by two readers) and defending or fixing each of the 53 report-only gate notes. Chapter-closing beats, interlude voice/length, artefact blockquotes and the verbatim recurring formulae were declared untouchable up front. | accepted |
| ADR-010 | 2026-08-03 | PDF engine — a portable `tectonic` on `D:` (path in gitignored `tools/local.ps1`) instead of a system TeX distribution: a full MiKTeX/TeX Live install would exhaust the small system drive, and the project's hard rule forbids writing to `C:`. `build.ps1` prefers any TeX engine on PATH and falls back to `$env:TECTONIC_HOME\tectonic.exe`, keeping the TeX bundle cache beside the engine. | accepted |
| ADR-011 | 2026-08-04 | Book output — EPUB (reader) + MOBI (Kindle, converted from the EPUB by Calibre) + A5 PDF (print/screen), all from one assembled markdown file, built by `powershell tools\build.ps1`. Cover generated programmatically by `tools/make_cover.py` (Pillow, no external assets, seeded RNG → reproducible; the title block auto-fits the clear column left of the seam, so a title change needs no layout work); front matter, colophon and metadata live in `book/`. Verified on the built artefacts, not the source: Czech diacritics, hyphenation, part titles and both framing sentences read back out of the PDF and the EPUB. | accepted |
| ADR-012 | 2026-08-04 | Final title — ***Den, který se nestal***, replacing the K0 working title *Nedoložení* (ADR-001). The author rejected the working title as odd-sounding and un-Czech, and he was right: `nedoložení` is a real word but lives only in officialese ("z důvodu nedoložení dokladů"), so as a title it reads as a fragment of a form and is hard to say aloud. The replacement was chosen from a four-way shortlist spanning readable-commercial, literary-personal, cold-institutional and quiet-SF registers; the author picked the most readable one. Chapter 15 keeps its own title.
